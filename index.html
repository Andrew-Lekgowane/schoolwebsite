FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Calvin Private School

Enrollment is open to all students in our district. Visit our office with proof of residency, birth certificate, immunization records, and previous school transcripts. You can also start the process online through our contact form.

We offer 15+ AP courses including AP Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Calculus AB/BC, Statistics, English Language & Literature, US History, World History, Government, Spanish, French, Computer Science, and Art History.

Regular school hours are 8:00 AM to 3:15 PM, Monday through Friday. Early bird classes start at 7:00 AM, and after-school activities run until 5:30 PM.

Yes, we provide free bus transportation for students living more than 1.5 miles from school. Bus routes and schedules are available on our website and in the main office.

We offer 50+ clubs and organizations including student government, debate team, robotics, drama, band, choir, various sports teams, National Honor Society, and many special interest clubs. There's something for everyone!

Follow us on social media, check our website regularly, sign up for our email newsletter, and download our mobile app for real-time notifications about events, closures, and important announcements.

We provide comprehensive support including academic counseling, college counseling, tutoring services, special education services, ESL programs, mental health counseling, and career guidance.

Yes, we have a reasonable dress code that promotes a positive learning environment. Clothing must be appropriate for school, free from offensive language or images, and meet basic modesty standards. Full details are in the student handbook.
